    How it works. The map starts from every City of Atlanta street block in the area. It removes blocks with a confirmed meter, the Midtown and Downtown meter districts, the Georgia Tech campus and Atlantic Station. Next it marks permit streets as time-limited, then removes highways, ramps, park drives and major roads, where curb parking isn’t allowed. Whatever is left is green.

    The city publishes no meter inventory. Block rules come from individually checked on-street listings, the Home Park and Ansley Park permit programs, Midtown’s permit streets, ATLPlus hours and GT Parking. Each block’s popup says whether its rule was confirmed or inferred. Green means no rule on record, not a guarantee. Rush-hour tow zones, street sweeping and game-day restrictions near Bobby Dodd still apply. Posted signs always win.
